Focus Alarm

Set up in a minute

When you spend too long on distracting sites, a full-screen alarm covers the page until you solve a few coding or computer-science questions. It is a commitment device, not a lock: you can always switch it off.

Quick start

You only need two things. 1. Check the list of sites in step 1 (the defaults are fine). 2. Click Allow all sites in step 4, then Save and finish at the bottom. Nothing is tracked until you allow site access. Everything else already has a sensible default and you can change it later in the popup.

1 What counts as a distraction

One site per line. Subdomains are included, so reddit.com also covers old.reddit.com. Anything not listed is never counted.

2 When the alarm rings

Time only counts while the browser is focused, you are not idle, and the active tab is on your list. A short glance at another tab does not reset your progress; five minutes away does. You get a limited number of snoozes (5, 10, 30 or 60 minutes) that you can change in the popup.

3 Take a break

Need a real rest, or watching a lecture on a listed site? Open the Focus Alarm popup and press Start break. The timer pauses: nothing is counted and no alarm rings until the break ends or you stop it. For YouTube you can also list study channels in the popup.

4 Site access

Chrome hides the address of the page you are on until you allow it. Choose how much to allow. You can change this later at chrome://extensions, Focus Alarm, Details, Site access.

All sites recommended

The alarm appears on whichever tab you switch to, so it cannot be dodged by changing tabs. Focus Alarm reads only the address of the active tab, to compare it with your list.

Only my list

Access is limited to the sites above. The alarm appears only on those sites; switching to an unlisted tab shows no overlay.

5 Privacy

  • Everything stays on this computer. There is no account, no server and no analytics.
  • The address of the active tab is compared with your list and then discarded. No browsing history is recorded or sent anywhere.
  • Settings, progress and simple counters (alarms, solved, snoozes) are saved in the browser's local extension storage and disappear if you remove the extension.

6 How to turn it off

  • Click the Focus Alarm icon and press the big switch. It works even while the alarm is ringing.
  • Or use the toggle, or Remove, on the extension's card at chrome://extensions.
  • If an alarm ever gets stuck, the overlay shows a recovery button after a failed load.
